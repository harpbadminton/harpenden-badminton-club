About Us

A Very Successful Badminton Club

Harpenden Badminton Club is a very successful club offering badminton to all ages. We are based at Westminster Lodge, Verulamium Park, St. Albans — playing on 4 courts every Tuesday evening.

Our members compete in teams (Men's 6 & 4, Ladies, Mixed) entered into both the Hertfordshire County League and the SW Herts League. This season, 2 of our teams are champions!

We currently have limited membership opportunities. Interested? Come along to our Tuesday evening club night as a guest to see how you get on.
