Notable Records

Highlights from the History Books

Medley Dominance

The Medley team holds the club's best all-time win rate at 75% — 82 wins from 110 matches, including a remarkable 47 wins from 56 home games.

SWHBL Ladies

The SWHBL Ladies team has a strong 58% win rate across 123 matches, with 71 wins and a home record of 49W–15D–36L.

SWHBL Men's

The SWHBL Men's team leads with 102 wins from 164 matches (62%) — the most wins of any single team in the club's history.

Home Fortress

At home the club has won 289 of 462 matches (63%) — a significantly stronger record than away (216 wins from 457, 47%).

HarpendenBadminton Club

A friendly and competitive badminton club based in St. Albans & Harpenden, Hertfordshire. Over 40 years of badminton.
