About Us

A Club with Over 40 Years of History

The club has been running for over 40 years. Its original home was in a small hall in Harpenden, but as the club expanded a new venue was sought and in 2006 we moved to St. Albans.

In February 2013 we moved to our present home at Westminster Lodge Sports Centre, Holywell Hill, St. Albans, AL1 2DL — which offers the best playing conditions available.

The regular season runs from the first Tuesday in September through to the end of May. Whilst the emphasis is on competitive badminton up to county league standard, members are keen to encourage the development of juniors and new members via playing competitively at club nights.

Since 2009 the club has played 919 competitive matches across all teams, winning 55% overall. View full history →

Winter Guests

Thinking of joining? Here's how it works.

Guest Fee

As a guest you are charged £12.50 for 2 hours. This fee is reimbursed if you go on to become a member.

Please confirm your attendance BEFORE you turn up — club nights are very busy.

Trial Evenings

You can attend up to 3 evenings, during which we assess your playing ability. If membership space is available, membership will be offered.

Coaching

We do not have the infrastructure to provide coaching, but we have links to another club who coach beginners, juniors & basic standard players.

Club Attitude

An important aspect of our club is 'attitude' — we play with a smile and enjoy our badminton. Club nights use a peg-board system to ensure a good mix of games throughout the evening.

Are You Inspired?

If you feel you can play badminton at the appropriate level and you...

  • Want to get back playing again
  • Want to get the body moving after lockdown
  • Have just moved into the area and are looking for a club to join to play regularly and competitively
  • Currently play for a club in the area but would like a change of venue and day

Then please drop us an email or call one of the club officials to arrange an evening for you to attend. We look forward to hearing from you!

From June through to the end of August we run a Summer Club that is open to all — young & old, county standard or those who just wish to carry on playing throughout the year.

It's a real opportunity to improve and keep fit during those warm summer evenings.
