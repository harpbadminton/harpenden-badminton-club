Club Tournaments

Year-by-year results from our annual inter-club tournaments — played between club members at the end of each season.

What are the Club Tournaments?

The Club Tournaments are inter-club events — played exclusively between members of Harpenden Badminton Club. They are not league matches against other clubs.

  • Held over five evenings in May, at the end of each regular season and before Summer Club begins
  • Open to all club members — from beginners to county-standard players
  • Run as handicapped events in Ladies, Mixed and Men's categories — so anyone can win
  • Winners are crowned Club Champions for that season
  • A highlight of the social calendar — competitive but played in great spirit
